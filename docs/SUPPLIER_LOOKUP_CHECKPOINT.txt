Behaviour
- Stock editor and new-material PDF review offer an independent Image source:
  Capral, Rondo, or Gyprock Trade. Source/query never become stock metadata.
- Rondo uses exact part-number matching against the product slug and heading,
  verified canonical page and its own image cell. Compatible-part mentions in
  search descriptions do not count as matches.
- Gyprock Trade is explicitly public Gyprock manufacturer PRODUCT-NAME lookup.
  Invoice codes and other manufacturers' ranges are not mapped. Search requires
  a matching official product label, canonical page and heading; images come
  only from that product's overview, excluding banners and related cards.
- The product's actual photo is preferred; associated bitmap diagrams remain
  available. Official page/image hosts and paths are separately allowlisted.
- Image download re-reads the exact product page and checks image membership.
  Different-product/asset redirects, unapproved hosts/paths, unsupported image
  formats, excessive responses and timeouts are rejected. TLS stays verified.
- Explicit Use image stages a decoded/optimised copy. Save or Import reviewed
  materials is required to persist it. PDF selection clears review confirmation.
- Source/query changes, changed items, cancellation, closing and replacement
  PDFs invalidate stale requests. Uncertain saves retain their exact retry image.
- Search/download do not mutate stock. Existing photos, codes, prices, suppliers
  and quantities are retained until an explicit save; imports start at zero.
