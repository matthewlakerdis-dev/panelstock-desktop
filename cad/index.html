1. Add a sketch

Upload a PDF or image, then highlight each panel by drawing a box around it. Selected panels are read automatically. Select multiple panels across pages, up to 30 per workspace. PDFs up to 25 MB; images up to 6 MB.

Reading sketches sends your selected panel areas or uploaded images to the configured OpenAI service to extract dimensions.

Drawing rules

Choose the code shown on the sketch for each edge. Dimensions are in millimetres.

B Big Tag

20 mm return with a route at the fold. Includes fixing holes and a physical fabrication tag.

S Small Tag

20 mm return with a route at the fold. Includes fixing holes and a physical fabrication tag.

NT Not Tag

20 mm return with a route at the fold. No fixing holes or physical tag piece in the summary.

RE Rolled Edge

20 mm return with a route at the fold. No fixing holes. Kept as RE on the drawing.

FE Factory edge

Follows the cut outline without adding a 20 mm return. No holes by default.

With tag: keeps the FE shape and label; adds B/S-style holes, 12 mm inward from the cut edge, and a fabrication tag in the summary.

CR Cap route

A separate dark-blue route, 0.2 mm outside the cut outline. No 20 mm return or fixing holes.

Holes & fabrication tags

Holes are 3 mm diameter with a maximum spacing of 300 mm. All hole centres sit 8 mm from the cut edge. End cuts, corners and fold reliefs can shorten or split the drilling span; spans too short for a safe pair of holes are omitted and flagged.

Physical tags extend 5 mm beyond the outer rim of each end hole. Separate uninterrupted spans receive separate pieces. Summary cut lengths round up to the next 5 mm.

Stiffeners & measurements

Stiffeners are spaced no more than 900 mm apart, with 50 mm clearance at FE and CR ends. Stiffener dimensions round to the nearest whole millimetre.

Entered sketch dimensions and measurement constraints drive the geometry. Finished dimensions include the applicable fold and edge deductions; drawing dimensions report the generated geometry.
