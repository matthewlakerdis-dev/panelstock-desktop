1. Add a sketch

Upload a PDF, then draw boxes around the panels you want to read. Select multiple panels across pages, up to 30 per workspace. PDFs up to 25 MB; images up to 6 MB.

Reading sketches sends your selected panel areas or uploaded images to the configured OpenAI service to extract dimensions.

Drawing rules

B / S / NT / RE tags: 20 mm. NT and RE have no holes. FE belongs to CUT. CR is separate, dark blue and 0.4 mm outside CUT.

Green cut · Red route · Dark blue cap route · Light blue holes

Rectangular and stepped panels with square perimeter corners are supported. Horizontal folds can span separate arms of stepped panels, ending at tagged vertical sides. Other geometry is flagged for review.

2. Review panel details

Follow the outline counterclockwise, starting along the bottom towards the right. Site measurements stay beside the proposed finished measurements.
